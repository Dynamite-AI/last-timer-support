Please review the information below before reporting an issue with notifications or purchase restoration.

Notifications do not arrive

In iPhone Settings, confirm that notifications are allowed for Last Timer and check whether Focus or Low Power Mode may be affecting delivery. The operating system may delay or omit notifications.

Restore a Pro purchase

Sign in to the store with the same Apple Account or Google Account used for the purchase, open Last Timer Pro in the App, and select Restore purchase.

Timers are missing after changing devices

Timers and history are stored on the device. The current version does not provide device-to-device sync or cloud backup.

Contact and issue reports

Use the public support form and include your device model, iOS or Android version, steps to reproduce, and expected result.

Because the form is public, do not include names, addresses, phone numbers, purchase identifiers, tax identifiers, or bank information.
