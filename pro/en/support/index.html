If notifications do not arrive, check notification permission for Last Timer Pro, Focus and Low Power Mode in iPhone/iPad Settings. The operating system may delay or omit notifications.

Export data from Information in the original Last Timer, then select the file in Data transfer in Pro’s Information screen. Import requires an app with no timers or history. Imported notifications are off; the original data and notifications remain. Check both apps before enabling notifications to avoid duplicate alerts.

Pro is a separate paid app, distinct from the Pro in-app purchase in Last Timer. Previous purchases do not transfer. Redownload it from the App Store using the Apple Account that bought it. Keep your exported transfer files safe.
